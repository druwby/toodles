# Pexels Stock Video — Search URLs

You need **2 video clips** for Scene 6 compositing. All Pexels video is free for commercial use, no attribution required.

## What you need

- **Clip 1:** one person, upper-body/headshot framing, appearing to look at a webcam, talking or smiling. 30+ sec. Gender/look doesn't matter — needs to feel like a college student.
- **Clip 2:** a different person from Clip 1, same framing criteria. Visual contrast with Clip 1 (different gender / different hair / different background) adds believability.

## Search URLs — open in browser

1. https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/woman%20video%20call/
2. https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/man%20webcam/
3. https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/student%20laptop/
4. https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/video%20call%20smile/
5. https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/face%20camera%20talk/

## Filter criteria (left sidebar on Pexels)

- **Orientation:** Portrait (closer to iPhone tile aspect ratio)
- **Duration:** 30 sec – 1 min
- **Size:** HD or 4K

## Downloading

1. Click a clip → preview. Does the person look natural, not obviously stock-posed?
2. Click the green **Free Download** button
3. Pick **1080p**, or 720p if 1080p is >100MB
4. Save to `D:\toodles-demo\assets\pexels-clip-A.mp4` and `pexels-clip-B.mp4`

## Avoid

- Green-screen / chromakey footage (looks fake under compositing)
- Obvious office attire (this is a dating app, not LinkedIn)
- Luxury settings (penthouse, yacht) — doesn't match "college student"
- Overly-produced clips with camera movement — you want static webcam-style
- Anything with on-screen text or watermarks

## Time budget

**30 minutes** to find 2 good clips. If you spend longer, pick the least-bad options and keep moving — the rest of the video carries the demo.
