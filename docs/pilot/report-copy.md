## Section 9.x: Informal Pilot

### Framing paragraph

> To gather formative feedback before v1.2 planning, we conducted a brief informal pilot over six days (2026-05-05 to 2026-05-10) with eight participants drawn from the authors' personal networks, six of whom are CSUF students. This was an informal feedback study; it was **not** an IRB-sanctioned research pilot and no inferential claims are made from the results. Participants were told the app was a capstone project, that their data would be deleted after 2026-05-31, and that paraphrased — not quoted — feedback might appear in this document.

## Things NOT to say in the report

These phrasings should be avoided — they're small enough to slip past casually but big enough to cost a Relevance or Traceability point if the grader catches them.

- ❌ "We conducted a CSUF student study with eight participants."
  - ✅ "We conducted an informal pilot with eight participants, six of whom were CSUF students."
- ❌ "78% of users preferred the interest-weighted algorithm."
  - ✅ Unless you have a control group, don't cite preference percentages. Paraphrase what they said.
- ❌ "Users reported higher trust in the platform due to the recovery path."
  - ✅ "Participants who engaged with the recovery path described it as [paraphrase what they actually said]."
- ❌ "Pilot results validate the v1.1 architecture."
  - ✅ "Pilot feedback surfaced [list] as priorities for v1.2; the v1.1 architecture accommodates these changes without rework."
