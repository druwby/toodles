# Recruit Template — Informal Pilot Invite

**Channel:** iMessage / SMS / Instagram DM. Personal, one-on-one. No mass-send.
**Length:** Short. The recipient should be able to decide in 20 seconds.
**Tone:** Ask, don't sell.

---

## Version A — for close friends

> Hey — I'm shipping my CSUF capstone this week. It's a 60-second video chat app for Fullerton students, and I need ~6 people to try it for 10 minutes and fill out a short form. You're one of the 10 I'm asking. No pressure, totally cool if you can't. If yes, I'll send the link and a quick form on Monday.

## Version B — for friends-of-friends / acquaintances

> Hey [name] — hope you're good. I'm wrapping up my CS capstone at CSUF and one of the deliverables is a small user pilot. 10 minutes on iPhone (or browser, I'll explain), a short feedback form after. Your feedback would genuinely help me. Interested?

## Version C — for the CS-adjacent crowd who want to hack on it

> Hey — capstone pilot going out next week. App is a 60-second video dating thing for Fullerton. Built it in SwiftUI + Firebase. I'd love honest feedback from someone who'll actually read the commit log before trying it. Down?

## Logistics reply (send after they say yes)

> Nice. Sending Monday 5/5. You'll need either iPhone (TestFlight link) or any browser (Appetize link). There's a Google Form after — ~10 questions, 3 minutes. I'll paraphrase your feedback in my capstone writeup; no direct quotes without your permission. Data gets deleted May 31.

## Things NOT to say

- ❌ "It could be the next Tinder!" — don't hype the product.
- ❌ "It's IRB-approved." — it isn't; don't imply otherwise.
- ❌ "It'll only take 2 minutes." — it's 10. Don't misrepresent.
- ❌ "Your feedback will be published." — paraphrased in a class writeup, not published.
